# Achilles — Business Model And Pricing

Detailed packaging, pricing, billing cadence, and contract terms for Achilles.

## 1. Positioning

Achilles is built primarily for enterprise buyers in regulated and operationally complex industries. The company target is an `80% Enterprise / 20% SMB` revenue mix, with enterprise as the core sales motion, core deployment shape, and core roadmap driver.

SMB exists as a feeder tier and expansion pool, not as the main company focus. The SMB offer should prove value quickly, generate expansion candidates, and avoid pulling product or support resources away from enterprise delivery.

Achilles automates large parts of the structural risk analysis work now done manually by risk, resilience, compliance, continuity, and consulting teams. It replaces substantial dependency-mapping, scenario-testing, and audit-preparation effort, while final risk ownership stays with the client.

## 2. Segmentation And Revenue Mix

### Enterprise

- Primary segment
- Target share: `80%` of revenue
- Buyer profile: regulated enterprises and operationally complex organizations
- Core wedge: banks, insurers, telecoms, utilities, healthcare, and critical infrastructure

### SMB / Growth

- Secondary segment
- Target share: `20%` of revenue
- Buyer profile: scaleups and growing companies that need structural risk visibility before building a full internal risk function
- Role in the portfolio: feeder tier for future enterprise upgrades

## 3. Pricing Principles

- Pricing is based primarily on `employee count`.
- The customer-facing list price should stay simple and predictable.
- Internal pricing adjustments can consider deployment type, legal-entity count, business-unit count, reporting requirements, support level, and graph complexity.
- All plans are sold on annual terms only.
- All standard plans are invoiced quarterly in advance.
- Longer commitments should be rewarded with a triennial discount, but the product should still be sold on business value rather than on discounting.

## 5. Package Definitions

### SMB Starter

**Target**

- `50-150 employees`

**Commercial rules**

- annual contract only
- billed quarterly in advance
- if canceled during the first quarter, the remaining three quarters of year one are not invoiced
- cloud only
- no SSO
- no on-prem
- no advanced compliance reporting

**Purpose**

- low-friction entry point
- founder, COO, or CTO-led purchase
- proof of value without enterprise delivery overhead

### Growth

**Target**

- `151-500 employees`

**Commercial rules**

- annual contract only
- billed quarterly in advance
- first-quarter exit rule available for new customers
- cloud only by default
- stronger exports and light role-based access

**Purpose**

- monetize upper SMB without diluting enterprise positioning
- create a clear upgrade path into enterprise packaging

### Enterprise Platform

**Target**

- `500+ employees`

**Commercial rules**

- annual contract only
- quarterly invoicing available by default
- no standard public first-quarter cancellation promise
- enterprise security review
- SSO / SCIM
- segmented data views
- compliance reporting
- on-prem or isolated deployment options
- SLA-backed support

**Purpose**

- price against consultant cost, manual internal effort, and compliance urgency
- support regulated procurement and long-term expansion

## 6. Billing Rules

### Annual Contract Policy

All plans are sold on annual terms only. Achilles does not offer month-to-month subscriptions.

### Quarterly Invoicing Policy

All standard plans are invoiced quarterly in advance. This keeps the contract annual while reducing upfront cash friction, especially for SMB and Growth customers.

### First-Quarter Exit Policy

This policy applies only to `SMB Starter` and `Growth` for new customers.

- customers sign a 12-month agreement
- billing occurs quarterly in advance
- if the customer decides during the first quarter that the product is not a fit, they may stop after quarter one
- in that case, the remaining three quarters of year one are not invoiced
- after the first quarter, the standard annual commitment applies

This gives smaller customers a low-risk first commitment without reintroducing true monthly pricing.

### Enterprise Cancellation Policy

Enterprise contracts remain annual commitments. Quarterly billing is available, but no standard first-quarter termination option is presented in the public pricing framework. Exceptions can be negotiated in enterprise MSAs or order forms where required.

## 7. Triennial Plan Rules

All tiers offer a 3-year option at `15% off` the equivalent 3 years of annual list price.

Triennial plans should be positioned as:

- budget-stability offers
- procurement-efficiency offers
- retention and planning tools for larger accounts

They should not be positioned as discount-first products.

Billing for triennial agreements may still be quarterly or annual depending on the negotiated invoicing structure.

### Triennial Rationale

- rewards longer commitment
- improves retention and revenue predictability
- fits enterprise procurement cycles

## 8. Enterprise Services And Add-Ons

These are separate from the annual license price:

- initial onboarding / first assessment: `€10,000-25,000`
- on-prem or isolated deployment setup: `€15,000-40,000`
- custom compliance report package: `€5,000-15,000`
- additional legal-entity rollout or business-unit expansion: custom scoped

Separating services from license pricing protects margin and keeps enterprise expansion clean.

## 9. Messaging Guidance

Use this message consistently:

- Achilles is enterprise-first.
- It automates structural risk analysis and replaces large parts of manual dependency mapping, scenario preparation, and audit-pack generation.
- It does not remove client accountability for risk ownership or decision-making.
- SMB is intentionally constrained and exists to prove value fast, not to mirror enterprise feature depth.

Avoid:

- positioning Achilles as a generic low-cost SMB tool
- implying that risk ownership is outsourced to the platform
- reintroducing monthly pricing language in sales or product copy

## 10. Qualification And Upgrade Rules

### Qualification

- `50-150 employees`: SMB Starter
- `151-500 employees`: Growth
- `500+ employees`: Enterprise Platform

Enterprise packaging can also be used below 500 employees when security, compliance, deployment, or organizational complexity justifies it.

### Upgrade Triggers

Move SMB or Growth accounts toward enterprise when they need:

- SSO / SCIM
- on-prem or isolated deployment
- compliance-aligned reporting
- multi-entity or segmented data handling
- SLA-backed support
- wider rollout across business units
