# Achilles — 3-Minute Jury Pitch

Use the six core slides below. Keep each slide light and speak the narrative around it. Target roughly 25 to 30 seconds per slide.

## Slide 1 — Problem

**Title:** Enterprise Risk Is Still Mapped Manually

**Slide text**

- Hidden dependencies create unseen risk
- Manual mapping is slow and expensive
- Disruption reveals problems too late

**Speaker note**

Large organizations run on hidden dependencies between teams, systems, products, and deployments. Today, understanding those dependencies is still manual, fragmented, and outdated quickly. That makes security, compliance, and business continuity risk far harder to manage than it should be.

## Slide 2 — Customer

**Title:** We Start Where Risk Is Expensive

**Slide text**

- Banks, telecoms, utilities, insurers
- Regulated enterprises with urgent pain
- NIS2, DORA, audits, continuity pressure

**Speaker note**

We start with enterprises where operational failure is expensive and regulation is rising. Banks, telecoms, utilities, and insurers already feel pressure from NIS2, DORA, internal audits, and business continuity requirements. These customers have both urgency and budget.

## Slide 3 — Solution

**Title:** A Deterministic Stress-Testing Platform

**Slide text**

- Map the organization team by team
- Build one graph of real dependencies
- Simulate cascade effects from failure or change

**Speaker note**

We build a deterministic organizational stress-testing platform. We work with the client team by team, using structured interviews and discovery to map departments, products, systems, and deployments as nodes in one operational graph. Once the graph exists, we can simulate how disruption or change spreads across the business.

## Slide 4 — Value

**Title:** Risk Mitigation First, Strategy Next

**Slide text**

- Better information creates better simulation
- Months of analysis reduced to hours
- Faster and safer operational decisions

**Speaker note**

This starts as a risk-management product, but it becomes a strategic tool. Once the organization has a reliable graph, decisions become faster and more grounded. Work that used to require long audits, workshops, and manual analysis can be compressed dramatically.

## Slide 5 — Go-To-Market

**Title:** Enterprise Sales, Security Review, Deployment

**Slide text**

- Licensing-based enterprise business model
- Sales meeting, then security review, then deployment
- Segmented data and on-premise options

**Speaker note**

We sell through enterprise sales. The process is straightforward: commercial meeting first, then security and operations review, then a scoped deployment project. For sensitive clients, we can support segmented data handling and on-premise delivery, which removes a major adoption blocker.

## Slide 6 — Traction

**Title:** First Pilot Fast, Then Expand

**Slide text**

- First win: operational risk and continuity
- Traction matters more than perfect scope
- Expand into audits, documents, and planning

**Speaker note**

Our immediate goal is to win the first pilot as fast as possible. We start with the clearest pain point: operational risk, security, and business continuity. Once we prove traction, we can expand into compliance support, internal process audits, document generation, and broader strategic simulations.

## Closing Line

Achilles helps enterprises move from manually guessing where they are fragile to deterministically seeing how failure spreads and what to fix first.
